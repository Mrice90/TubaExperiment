# Rules questions

Resolved: five-card starting-player hand and six-card other-player hand; Player 1 starts with 0 GP and Player 2 with 1 GP; coin-flip initiative; player-selected Capital placement; combined 4×6 battlefield; diagonal movement/range costs one space; losing all Lands, Structures and Capitals causes defeat; persistent GP generated by Capitals, Lands, and Structures; turn-gated free development cards; Start Phase draw and untap; split movement; exact Defense kills with simultaneous retaliation; opportunity attacks; and 1-damage exhaustion.

1. When inserting beneath a Structure, may the player choose any legal stack depth?
2. If both players lose their last Permanent in the same resolving effect, is the result a draw?
3. What are base HP values for Lands, Structures and Capitals when not printed?
4. Are tokens removed at the start of their controller's turn or their creator's turn?
