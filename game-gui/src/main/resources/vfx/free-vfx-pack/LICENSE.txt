Files in this directory are single frames extracted (no pixel edits, only
PNG re-encoding) from the pack's own animated GIF previews, fetched via the
korlibs/korge-free-gfx mirror which republishes the pack under CC0
(https://github.com/korlibs/korge-free-gfx/tree/HEAD/CodeManuPro):

  vortex_swirl.png   - frame 19 (0-based) of the 48-frame Effect_TheVortex.gif
                       (cyan spiral vortex). The frame's luminance was baked
                       into its alpha channel (white RGB) so the game's
                       silhouette-tint pipeline preserves the spiral arms when
                       the swirl is tinted the summoning faction's color.
                       Used as the summon-arrival swirl.
  ember_debris_a.png - frame 26 (0-based) of the 41-frame Effect_Explosion.gif
                       (chunky fire clusters). Destroy-debris variant A.
  ember_debris_b.png - frame 32 (0-based) of the 44-frame Effect_Explosion2.gif
                       (scattered ember ring). Destroy-debris variant B.
  ember_debris_c.png - frame 27 (0-based) of the 44-frame Effect_Explosion2.gif
                       (fire ring with embers). Destroy-debris variant C.
